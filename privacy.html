Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

HomeLinq is a trading name of Infinite Life Records Ltd (Company No. 11357335). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data responsibly in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who We Are

Infinite Life Records Ltd is the data controller for personal data processed through the HomeLinq platform. Our registered office is in England and Wales.

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at privacy@homelinq.co.uk.

2. What Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, postal address
  • Engineer details: Gas Safe registration number, business name, qualifications, service areas, callout rates
  • Customer details: property address, boiler information, job history
  • Payment data: processed securely by Stripe — we do not store card numbers
  • Usage data: how you interact with the platform, device info, IP address
  • Communications: messages between customers and engineers, support queries
  • Imported records: customers, jobs, invoices, gas safety certificates and expenses that an engineer chooses to import from another system or spreadsheet

For records an engineer creates or imports about their own customers, the engineer decides what is kept and why; HomeLinq stores and processes those records on the engineer's behalf.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data for the following purposes:

  • Providing and managing the HomeLinq platform and services
  • Matching customers with Gas Safe registered engineers
  • Processing payments via Stripe Connect
  • Sending service-related emails (invoices, booking confirmations, reminders)
  • Verifying engineer Gas Safe registration status
  • Providing Moti, our AI assistant, which helps engineers look up their records, prepare invoices and reminders, and import their records from other systems
  • Improving our platform and user experience
  • Complying with legal obligations (tax records, gas safety regulations)

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data under the following legal bases:

  • Contract: to provide our services when you create an account or book an engineer
  • Legitimate interests: to improve our platform, prevent fraud, and ensure safety
  • Legal obligation: to comply with gas safety regulations and tax requirements
  • Consent: for optional marketing communications (you can opt out at any time)

5. Data Sharing

We share your data only with:

  • Stripe: for payment processing (subject to Stripe's Privacy Policy)
  • Supabase: cloud database hosting (data stored in EU/UK data centres)
  • Resend: for transactional email delivery
  • Anthropic: provides the AI model behind Moti (subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy). See "Moti and AI processing" below.
  • Engineers/Customers: contact details shared only when a booking is confirmed

We never sell your personal data to third parties.

Moti and AI processing

  • Moti chat: when an engineer asks Moti something, the message and the records Moti looks up to answer it (for example job, invoice or certificate details) are sent to Anthropic to generate the reply. Anything that changes data or sends an email is only carried out after the engineer confirms it.
  • Photos sent to Moti: when an engineer sends Moti a photo (for example a receipt, a boiler data plate or an old certificate), the photo is sent to Anthropic so Moti can read it. It is only kept if the engineer chooses to attach it to a job, in which case it is stored privately in their account.
  • Importing records: files an engineer imports are read on their own device. Moti receives only the column headings, a statistical summary of each column (for example how full it is, number and date ranges, and counts of values such as "Paid" or "Unpaid") and a few sample rows in which names, email addresses, phone numbers and addresses are hidden. The full file is never sent to Moti. The records themselves are saved to the engineer's HomeLinq account only when the engineer presses Import.
  • Import formats: when an import finishes, we keep the column headings of the file and how they were matched (no customer details) so the same export format can be recognised straight away next time, including for other engineers.
  • Parts and prices: parts and prices an engineer records (by hand or from a receipt read by Moti) are kept in their account. Unless they switch it off in Settings, prices are also used, without any names, account numbers, customers or jobs, to show anonymous price ranges once 3 or more engineers have priced the same part number.
  • Online part prices: when an engineer checks online prices, only the part name, maker and part number are sent to Anthropic, which searches a fixed list of UK merchants' websites. Results (public prices and links) are kept for 24 hours and shared between engineers; nothing about the engineer, their customers or jobs is included.
  • Import history: we keep a record of each import (file names, counts and progress) so an engineer can finish an interrupted import or undo it.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. After account deletion:

  • Personal data is deleted within 30 days
  • Financial records are retained for 7 years (HMRC requirement)
  • Gas safety certificates are retained for 2 years (Gas Safety Regulations 1998)

7. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data (Subject Access Request)
  • Rectify inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erase your data (right to be forgotten)
  • Restrict processing of your data
  • Port your data to another service (available via Settings → Export)
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent for marketing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@homelinq.co.uk. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

HomeLinq uses essential cookies only for authentication and session management. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. No cookie consent banner is required as we only use strictly necessary cookies.

9. Security

We protect your data with:

  • TLS/SSL encryption for all data in transit
  • Row-level security on our database
  • Stripe PCI-DSS compliance for payment data
  • Regular security reviews and access controls

10. Children

HomeLinq is not intended for use by individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
